# Chapter Three: Arrival

At noon on that same Monday, the cutter arrived with exactly four passengers. These were the same people who had departed at sunrise.
Ivo stepped ashore in his blue coat.
Mara rescued two newly arrived children from the southbound wagon; neither child was among the four evacuees sent north.
Mara's final tally said: "Of the original four evacuees, two are now rescued and only two remain missing."
Tess could find no clock or witness to confirm whether the tower bell had rung at six.
