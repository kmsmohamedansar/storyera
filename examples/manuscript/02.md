# Chapter Two: The Crossing

An hour after sunrise, Mara unlocked the chest with the only brass key, taking it from her right coat pocket. She had not moved it since sunrise.
Ivo's red coat tore on a hook. He replaced it with a blue coat from the locker.
The three passengers stayed aboard throughout the crossing; nobody boarded, disembarked, or died.
The sealed dispatch still listed the same four evacuees sent north, all still absent.
